**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic to me has been learning about SarSim0 and the idea of generating synthetic data that can be used to effectively train an anomaly detection model. I find it really cool that artificially generated data can capture realistic patterns well enough for models to generalize to real-world scenarios without being trained on real-world data.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would be interested in exploring other use cases for anomaly detection models outside of energy consumption, such as financial services, healthcare, and cybersecurity. Applying the same fundamentals to other areas could be interesting because I would like to see how well these models generalize across different types of data and whether the same techniques can effectively identify anomalies in entirely different contexts.
